Local Superfoods Guide

Malunggay (Moringa)

  • ✓ 7× more Vitamin C than oranges
  • ✓ 4× more Calcium than milk
  • ✓ 3× more Iron than spinach

Kamote (Sweet Potato)

  • ✓ Rich in Vitamin A (beta-carotene)
  • ✓ Good source of Vitamin C
  • ✓ Supports immune function

Monggo (Mung Beans)

  • ✓ High plant-based protein
  • ✓ Rich in folate for brain development
  • ✓ Affordable and widely available

Feeding Tip from WHO Guidelines

For children 6–8 months, start with 2–3 meals per day of thick porridge and mashed foods. Increase to 3–4 meals per day for children 9–23 months, adding snacks as desired. Always wash hands before preparing food and feeding your child.
